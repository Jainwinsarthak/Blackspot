# Three-minute demo script

**0:00–0:25 — Problem.** “India loses roughly 170,000 people to road crashes every year. The data tells us where people died; BlackSpot helps teams prevent the next fatal crash.”

**0:25–0:55 — Map.** “This is Delhi. Every extruded column aggregates segment-level risk from road geometry, lighting, junction complexity and historical patterns. Red is where a fatal crash is most likely.” Hover and select a red road.

**0:55–1:25 — Explain.** “This segment is risky for concrete reasons: missing median protection, curvature, speed, and low sight distance. The factor chart makes this auditable rather than a black box.”

**1:25–2:05 — Scenario.** Enable Rain and Night. “Under monsoon rain at night, risk rises instantly across the city. The counter identifies additional critical segments before an operational shift begins.”

**2:05–2:40 — Intervention.** Select “Add median barrier” and “Add traffic signal,” then simulate. “This is the action layer: the model estimates the score after infrastructure changes, turning a hotspot map into a prioritisation tool.”

**2:40–3:00 — Trust and close.** “There is no runtime LLM or cloud model call. The models are local, the data fallback is bundled, and BlackSpot is deployable for a stable field demo today.”
